Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

The short version: Snowy stores as little as it can: your Discord ID, the few things you deliberately save, like a reminder or your timezone, and a log of the commands you run that only the bot's owner can see. It doesn't sell anything, show ads or track you. Some commands send what you type to outside services, which are listed below.

Who runs Snowy

Snowy is a free hobby project made and run by satu. It isn't a company. If you have a question about your data, see Contact at the bottom.

What Snowy stores

Snowy keeps a small database on the computer it runs on. It only saves something when you use a command that needs it:

  • Reminders (/tools remindme): your Discord ID, the channel, the reminder text and when it's due. Each reminder is deleted once it's been sent, or when you remove it with /tools delreminder.
  • AFK status (/social afk): your Discord ID, your AFK message and when you set it. It's deleted the next time you send a message.
  • Timezone (/social settimezone): your Discord ID and the timezone you picked. It's kept until you remove it with /social cleartimezone.
  • Bot bans: if you're banned from using Snowy, it keeps your Discord ID, the reason, who banned you and when the ban ends. Temporary bans are deleted when they expire.
  • Command log: every command you run is logged to a private Discord channel that only the bot's owner can see. Each entry has your Discord username and ID, the server (name and ID) or DM it was used in, the channel, everything you typed into the command, whether it worked and how long it took. Each command also gets an automatic "danger" score, worked out from what it does, what you typed and how often you're using the bot, so the owner can spot abuse. There's no fixed time limit on how long entries are kept. The owner can delete them, and /social forgetme doesn't remove them.

That's everything. Snowy doesn't build a profile of you, and the command log is only used to keep the bot working and stop abuse.

What Snowy sees but doesn't keep

  • Messages. In the servers Snowy has joined, it reads messages to notice commands that start with -- and to tell people when someone they mentioned is AFK. Messages that aren't commands aren't saved anywhere; commands are kept in the command log above. When you use Snowy through Discord's user install, it only sees the commands you run.
  • Files and links you give it. Images, videos and audio sent to commands like /media are downloaded, processed and deleted as soon as the result is sent. Links are visited to do what the command says, then forgotten.
  • Error logs. If a command crashes, technical details are printed to the console of the computer running the bot so it can be fixed. These aren't sent anywhere else.

Who else sees what you send

Many commands work by asking another service for something. When you use one, that service receives what the command needs, such as a website address, a search term or a file. It doesn't receive your Discord account details.

  • A network proxy. Requests to websites you choose go through a proxy service, so those websites see the proxy's address instead of the bot's. The proxy provider can see which addresses are being visited.
  • VirusTotal (/virustotal and /safety linkcheck). Files and links you scan are sent to VirusTotal, which is owned by Google. VirusTotal shares submitted files and links with its security community, so don't scan anything private or personal.
  • Web and lookup services, including the Internet Archive (Wayback Machine), Google DNS, ip-api, RDAP, crt.sh, CertSpotter, HackerTarget, Anubis, the YouTube Data API, Radio Browser, popcat.xyz, TheCatAPI, http.dog, GDBrowser, Newgrounds, Roblox, Rolimons, WEAO (weao.xyz), Steam, computernewb and the meme video archive. Each one only receives the query for the command you ran.
  • Discord, which carries every message and command. Discord's own Privacy Policy applies to that.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. Snowy never sells or trades your information, and it doesn't use analytics or advertising.

Your choices

  • See your reminders with /tools reminders and remove one at any time with /tools delreminder.
  • Your AFK status clears itself as soon as you talk again.
  • Remove your timezone with /social cleartimezone.
  • Delete everything at once with /social forgetme. It removes your reminders, AFK status and timezone after you confirm. Bot bans and the command log aren't removed this way. To have your command log entries deleted, get in touch using the details below.
  • For anything else, get in touch using the details below.
  • To stop using Snowy, remove it from your account (Discord settings, then Authorized Apps) or from your server.

Age

You need to be old enough to use Discord in your country to use Snowy. Snowy doesn't knowingly store information about anyone younger than that.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, open an issue on the Snowy repository on Tianjo or message satu on Discord.
